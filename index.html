THE ENGINE UNDER THE HUD

The interface is flashy; the underlying model stays transparent so the project can be explained in a science or computing presentation.

01 / DRAG FORCE

Atmospheric particles collide with the spacecraft and remove orbital energy. Density and effective cross-sectional area are the biggest visual levers.

D = ½ · Cd · ρ · A · v²

02 / ORBITAL ENERGY

For a near-circular orbit, combining orbital energy with drag gives a differential equation for the semimajor axis.

da/dt = −(CdρA/m) · √(μa)

03 / ATMOSPHERE

The thermosphere is not uniform. This demo uses an exponential interpolation table and scales it for low, moderate or high solar activity.

ρ(h) ≈ ρ₀ · exp(−Δh/H)
